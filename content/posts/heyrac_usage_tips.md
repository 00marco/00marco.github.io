# 📊 A Gentle Tool for Managing Your Social Media Use — Without Going Cold Turkey
Let’s be honest: quitting social media cold turkey is hard.

For many of us, platforms like YouTube, Reddit, TikTok, or Instagram aren’t just distractions — they’re ways to unwind, learn, or feel connected. But when the line between useful and overwhelming starts to blur, it’s time to take a step back and look clearly at our habits.

That’s where this simple browser extension comes in.

✅ What It Does
This tool doesn’t block anything. It doesn’t shame you. It simply shows you the truth: how much time you're spending on different websites, and when you’re spending it.

🕒 Track Time on All Websites
Want to know how much of your day goes to browsing in general? The tool gives you a full timeline — no fluff, no judgment.

🔍 Zoom in on Specific Sites
Curious how long Reddit really holds your attention? Or how many hours YouTube eats up at night? You can filter by domain to see your usage for just that site.

📆 Time-of-Day Filters
Are you telling yourself you only scroll before bed? This feature lets you check that. Filter usage by time range to track whether your habits match your intentions.

🧾 Export to PDF
With one click, you can save a snapshot of your usage. Perfect if you're working with a coach, therapist — or just keeping yourself accountable.

🔒 Privacy First
Your data never leaves your device. There’s no tracking, no syncing, no cloud. It’s yours and yours alone.

## 🧠 Why It Helps with Social Media Addiction
Psychologists studying behavior change often recommend starting with awareness — not cutting yourself off immediately.

Two helpful strategies supported by behavioral science are:

Track how much time you’re spending
Just measuring your usage, day by day, helps you spot patterns you may not even realize. It brings habits into the light — and that awareness alone can reduce compulsive use.

Limit usage to specific time windows
Instead of banning social media outright, create a rule: “I only scroll between 7–9pm.” This tool helps you monitor how often you follow or break that rule, without judgment — just data.

Both strategies work best when they’re easy and automatic — exactly what this tool is designed for.

## 💬 Start Where You Are
If you’re feeling overwhelmed by social media or just want to reclaim a bit of focus in your day, this tool offers a simple first step: pay attention without punishment.

Install it. Let it run quietly for a few days. Then take a look.

You might be surprised — and more importantly, empowered to make changes that actually stick.
